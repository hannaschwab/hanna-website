# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The site serves three audiences equally:

- Event organizers evaluating Hanna for talks, workshops, and panels.
- Data and AI decision-makers considering consulting or collaboration.
- Technical peers and community members looking for practical writing, events, and projects.

## Product Purpose

The site is Hanna Schwab's professional home. It should generate speaking invitations and consulting leads while building authority through technical writing and community work. Success means visitors can quickly understand Hanna's expertise, verify her experience, and choose a relevant next step.

## Positioning

Hanna combines hands-on Microsoft Fabric and AI delivery with public speaking and community leadership. She is a Microsoft Foundry MVP, Data & AI Lead, international speaker, and founder of several technical communities.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically arrive from search, LinkedIn, conference programs, community events, or direct referrals. They may be checking credibility before making contact, looking for a suitable talk, or reading technical material.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js website with homepage, About, Speaking, Blog, Projects, and Contact routes.
- Speaking data, project data, certifications, and MDX blog content are maintained in the repository.
- The contact form sends inquiries through Resend.
- The redesign must preserve existing content and functionality.
- The site must work well on mobile and desktop.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Hanna Schwab.
- Voice: direct, practical, opinionated, and free of inflated marketing language.
- The existing visual identity is not binding; a full visual reset is allowed.

## Evidence on Hand

- Official Microsoft Foundry MVP profile.
- Conference and community event history in `data/talks.ts`.
- Certifications and community projects in `data/projects.ts`.
- Event and speaking photography in `public/`.
- Technical writing in `content/blog/`.
- No testimonials or client case studies are currently available and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Give speaking, consulting, and technical authority equal weight.
- Lead with specific evidence rather than generic claims.
- Make every audience's next step obvious without turning the site into a sales funnel.
- Keep content current and factual.
- Sound like a knowledgeable person, not a corporate brand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site should meet WCAG 2.2 AA expectations, support keyboard and assistive-technology use, preserve readable contrast, and work across common mobile and desktop viewport sizes.
